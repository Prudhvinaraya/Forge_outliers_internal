# ECI EVM Corpus Card

## Overview

This collection contains five Election Commission of India documents about Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) and the Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail (VVPAT). It combines the 2026 Manual on Electronic Voting Machine and a 2026 ECI-EVM FAQ corpus with brochures for presiding officers, electors, and candidates or political parties published from 2023 to 2024.

The documents serve different audiences. Polling officials use the manual and presiding-officer guidance for storage, First Level Checking (FLC), randomization, commissioning, mock polling, actual polling, replacement, counting, and custody. Candidates and political parties use the transparency and custody procedures to observe checks, sign seals, follow transport, monitor strong rooms, and participate at counting. Electors use the voter brochure and FAQ material to understand how the Ballot Unit (BU), Control Unit (CU), and VVPAT work, including accessibility and vote-verification features.

Core vocabulary includes EVM, BU, CU, VVPAT, Power Pack, EMS, Form 17A, Form 17C, mock poll, FLC, commissioning, randomization, and Rule 49MA. The corpus is procedural and documentary: it supports questions about EVM/VVPAT operation, safeguards, responsibilities, and election-process records. It does not contain live election results, voter-registration records, personal voter data, current vehicle telemetry, credentials, CCTV recordings, firmware source code, procurement data, or future election schedules. The collection therefore requires abstention when a question asks for unavailable operational or out-of-domain information.
